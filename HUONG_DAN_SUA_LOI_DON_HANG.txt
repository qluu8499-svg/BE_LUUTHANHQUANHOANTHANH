HUONG DAN BAN DA RA SOAT - BE4 QUAN LY DON HANG

1. Cac diem da sua trong source
- Customer OrdersController: tim kiem lich su don hang theo ma don HOAC ten san pham.
- Admin OrdersController: tim kiem don theo ma don, ten/SDT/email khach hang HOAC ten san pham.
- CartController.OrderSuccess: nap OrderDetails va Product bang Include de trang xac nhan co du du lieu.
- Views/Cart/OrderSuccess.cshtml: hien thi cac dong san pham, so luong, thanh tien va lien ket lich su mua hang.
- Giu dung ten cot AddressDelivery theo EDMX hien tai de tranh loi runtime khi DB chua migration.

2. Cac chuc nang va duong dan
- Khach hang xem lich su: /Orders
- Khach hang xem chi tiet: /Orders/Details/{id}
- Admin xem tat ca don: /Admin/Orders
- Admin xem chi tiet: /Admin/Orders/Details/{id}
- Admin cap nhat trang thai: form trong trang chi tiet admin.
- Khach hang checkout: /Cart/Checkout

3. Doi chieu voi PowerPoint
- Da bao dam luong tao Order + OrderDetail, xac nhan don va xem lich su/chi tiet.
- Admin co danh sach tat ca don hang, thong tin khach hang, chi tiet san pham va cap nhat trang thai.
- PowerPoint mo ta them DeliveryMethod, PaymentMethod, ShippingAddress va OrderDetail.TotalPrice computed.
  EDMX/DB trong ZIP hien tai van dang dung AddressDelivery va chua co cac cot tren.
  Khong tu dong doi ten cot hay them cot vao DB vi viec do can migration tren dung database MyStore
  va thao tac Update Model from Database trong Visual Studio. Cac view/controller hien tai tiep tuc
  dung schema hien co de tranh loi Invalid column name khi chay tren DB cu.

4. Cach kiem thu tren may Windows / Visual Studio
- Backup database MyStore.
- Mo BE4_LUUTHANHQUAN.sln, Restore NuGet Packages, Build > Rebuild Solution.
- Kiem tra connection string MyStoreEntities trong Web.config.
- Dam bao tai khoan admin co Users.UserRole = 'A' (hoac 'Admin').
- Dang nhap customer, them san pham vao gio, checkout; kiem tra bang Order va OrderDetail.
- Vao /Orders, tim theo ma don va ten san pham; mo chi tiet don.
- Dang nhap admin, vao /Admin/Orders; tim theo ma don/ten khach/ten san pham; mo chi tiet va doi trang thai.
- Dang nhap customer khac va thu mo URL chi tiet don cua customer dau tien: phai bi tu choi (404).

5. Gioi han xac minh
- Da kiem tra cu phap XML cua Web.config, cac web.config, csproj, packages.config va EDMX.
- Da kiem tra tat ca Compile Include trong csproj deu co tep tuong ung.
- Moi truong thao tac khong co MSBuild/Visual Studio va SQL Server, nen chua the Build C#,
  chay website hoac kiem thu truy van voi database thuc te. Can chay cac buoc o muc 4 truoc khi nop.
