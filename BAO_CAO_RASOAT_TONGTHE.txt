BAO CAO RA SOAT TONG THE - BE4_LUUTHANHQUAN
Ngay: 17/11/2026

A. CAC LOI CU THE DA PHAT HIEN VA SUA

1. Web.config - cau hinh bindingRedirect khong hop le
- Ban goc co nhieu khoi <dependentAssembly> chi chua <bindingRedirect>, thieu <assemblyIdentity>.
- Da thay bang cac redirect co assemblyIdentity tuong ung voi cac thu vien project dang dung: MVC, Razor/WebPages, Helpers, Newtonsoft.Json, EntityFramework, Antlr, Microsoft.Web.Infrastructure, System.Web.Optimization va WebGrease.
- Them customErrors mode="RemoteOnly" de khong hien chi tiet loi server cho truy cap tu xa.
- Giu nguyen connection string MyStoreEntities va cac gia tri PayPal placeholder; khong tu y thay doi thong tin moi truong.

2. PayPal - thieu view bao loi
- PaypalController goi View("FailureView") trong cac nhanh callback/giao dich that bai.
- Source khong co Views/Paypal/FailureView.cshtml, co the gay them HTTP 500 khi can hien thi trang that bai.
- Da bo sung view that bai, thong bao ro don hang chua tao va giu gio hang de thu lai; da khai bao Content trong csproj.

3. Admin/Products/Create - thieu partial view
- Create.cshtml goi PVCreateEditProduct nhung file partial khong ton tai, co the gay HTTP 500 khi mo trang them san pham.
- Da bo sung Areas/Admin/Views/Products/PVCreateEditProduct.cshtml va khai bao Content trong csproj.
- Sua markup class va thuoc tinh TextAreaFor bi viet sai trong partial hien co va partial moi.

4. Phan trang - page=0/page am co the gay exception
- Cac action Home/Index, Home/ProductList, Home/ProductDetails, Admin/Products/Index va Admin/Orders/Index dua truc tiep page tu query string vao PagedList.
- Da gioi han page toi thieu la 1 de tranh loi chi so trang khong hop le.

5. Tai nguyen Entity Framework
- HomeController tao MyStoreEntities nhung khong Dispose.
- Da bo sung Dispose(bool) de giai phong DbContext sau request.

B. KIEM TRA STATIC DA THUC HIEN
- Parse XML: Web.config, cac config, csproj, EDMX va cac file XML lien quan: khong phat hien loi cu phap.
- Kiem tra cac Compile/Content/None/EntityDeploy Include trong csproj: cac duong dan duoc khai bao deu ton tai.
- Kiem tra HintPath NuGet: cac DLL duoc tham chieu deu co tep tai duong dan khai bao.
- Kiem tra cac View/PartialView duoc goi bang ten tu Controller: khong con view/partial tham chieu bi thieu trong cac truong hop da quet.
- Kiem tra Layout va Html.Partial/RenderPartial: khong con partial tham chieu bi thieu trong cac view da quet.
- Kiem tra cac view .cshtml: tat ca deu duoc khai bao trong csproj.
- Khong sua schema database, khong chay SQL/migration va khong xoa bang/cot.

C. CAC MUC CHUA THE XAC NHAN
- Moi truong hien tai khong co MSBuild/Visual Studio/.NET Framework compiler; chua the Build/Rebuild that.
- Khong co SQL Server MyStore de ket noi thu; chua the xac nhan connection string, schema thuc te, quyen truy cap va du lieu.
- Connection string hien tai van la localhost\\SQLExpress, database MyStore, Windows Integrated Security. Can dam bao may chay co dung SQL Server instance/database va tai khoan IIS/IIS Express co quyen.
- PayPal Client ID/Secret van la YOUR_PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID / YOUR_PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET. Can thay bang Sandbox credentials truoc khi thu thanh toan.
- PayPal hien gui currency USD trong khi ProductPrice lay truc tiep tu database. Can xac nhan gia trong DB la USD; neu la VND thi phai thong nhat quy tac quy doi truoc khi bat thanh toan.
- Cac tinh nang phu thuoc schema ton kho/so luong ban va cot mo rong chua duoc them tu dong; khong chay DATABASE_ADD_INVENTORY.sql.

D. KIEM THU TREN WINDOWS
1. Giai nen ban ZIP, mo BE4_LUUTHANHQUAN.sln bang Visual Studio 2019/2022 co workload ASP.NET and web development.
2. Restore NuGet Packages, chon Build > Rebuild Solution.
3. Kiem tra Web.config: SQL Server instance/database MyStore, quyen Windows Authentication va PayPal Sandbox credentials.
4. Chay website, thu trang chu, danh sach/chi tiet san pham, dang ky/dang nhap.
5. Thu Admin: /Admin/Products/Create, /Admin/Products/Edit, /Admin/Orders, /Admin/Customers.
6. Thu Customer: gio hang, checkout COD, xem lich su don hang, chi tiet va mua lai.
7. Thu PayPal Sandbox ca luong thanh cong, cancel, callback thieu session va callback that bai.
8. Thu URL phan trang page=0 va page=-1; phai tu dong ve trang 1.

KET LUAN
Da sua cac loi source xac dinh duoc khi ra soat tinh trang project va bo sung cac view/partial bi thieu. Day la ket qua sua va kiem tra tinh toan ven static; chua the cam ket het HTTP 500 tren may chay that khi chua Build bang Visual Studio va chua ket noi dung SQL Server/PayPal cua ban.
